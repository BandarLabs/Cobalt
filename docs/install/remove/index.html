Remove Cobalt

This takes the Cobalt programs off a Kobo plugged into your computer, and takes Cobalt out of the reader's menu. Your books, settings and the applications with their data all stay where they are.

This browser cannot write to the reader.

  1. Connect the reader

    Plug the Kobo in and tap Connect on its screen, then choose the drive called KOBOeReader.

  2. Take Cobalt off

    Removes the Cobalt programs and its menu entry. NickelMenu itself is left alone, because other mods use it, and so is any menu entry that is not Cobalt's.

  3. Eject and restart

    Eject the drive before unplugging, then hold the power button until the reader restarts.

Other ways to remove it

Terminal If Cobalt was installed from one

kobo setup --undo

Terminal Otherwise

curl -fsSL https://bandarlabs.github.io/Cobalt/uninstall.sh | sh

Needs nothing installed first.
