Connect Lichess
Play Lichess games, challenge players, solve puzzles, or play offline.

Open in your browser:
  https://lichess.org/account/oauth/token/create?scopes[]=board:play&description=Cobalt

Then run:
  kobo secret set lichess --from <token-file> --device <address>
  The secret comes from a private file and is never shown here.

Full setup: kobo apps setup lichess
