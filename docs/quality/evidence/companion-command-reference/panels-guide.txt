Read comics added from your computer or Komga library.

Bundled guide: version 0.1.4

What the app can use:
  - Connects to online services

Setup:
1. Choose Try a sample to read offline, or Add comic for USB import instructions. CBZ files up to 32 MB are supported; CBR and PDF are not.
2. To use Komga, choose Browse and enter your HTTPS server address. Add your username and password in Account details, then check the connection.
3. Open Download on your shelf to retry a paused transfer. A completed download can be added to the shelf offline; saved comics remain available.

This guide does not check installation or account access.
