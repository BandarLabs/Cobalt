# A session rather than a game: two players play one out, the score is kept,
# the next game starts, and the Kobo takes the other side.
#
# Taps go to the squares by name rather than by coordinate. A route written in
# pixels re-breaks every time a line of text is added above the board, which is
# a test of the layout arithmetic rather than of the game.

tap Rules
wait-for Make three in a row
expect O goes first.

clean
