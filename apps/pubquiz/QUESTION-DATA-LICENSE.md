# Question data license

Pub Quiz has two kinds of question content.

## Bundled questions

The questions in `assets/questions.tsv` ship with the app. They were written
for Pub Quiz, are not taken from Open Trivia DB, and are licensed with the rest
of this repository (AGPL-3.0-only).

## Downloaded packs

Get more questions downloads a pack from [Open Trivia DB](https://opentdb.com/).
Open Trivia DB content is credited and redistributed under CC BY-SA 4.0. This
file accompanies cached packs as their attribution and license notice, and the
app stores the same notice on the reader beside the cached pack.
