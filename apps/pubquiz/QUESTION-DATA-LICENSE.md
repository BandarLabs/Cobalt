# Question data license

Pub Quiz has two kinds of question content.

## Bundled questions

The questions in `assets/questions.tsv` ship with the app. They were written
for Pub Quiz, are not taken from Open Trivia DB, and are licensed with the rest
of this repository (AGPL-3.0-only).

## Downloaded packs

**Get more questions** downloads a pack of 50 questions from
[Open Trivia DB](https://opentdb.com/). Open Trivia DB content is credited and
redistributed under CC BY-SA 4.0. This file is the attribution and license
notice for cached packs, and the app stores a short attribution notice on the
reader beside the cached pack.
