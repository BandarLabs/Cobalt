# Crossword captures

Actual SDK app output from the Clara BW simulator, taken with `kobo drive
--ideal`. `crossword-play.png` shows the play screen with Undo, Clues and the
direction switch under the grid; `clue-entry.png` and `crossword-entry.png`
keep the clue with entry; `crossword-menu.png` shows More with New game first;
`crossword-midi.png` is a 7×7 puzzle; `completed.png` shows a solved puzzle.
`save-recovery.png` shows the storage-full recovery route. These are simulator
captures, not a claim of completed physical-reader acceptance.
